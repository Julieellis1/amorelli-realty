01 — Our Story

Powerful & proven since 1978.

Amorelli Realty has been a staple in Astoria, Queens for over 40 years. In 1978 Anita Halvatzis purchased the company and, with the help of her two sons Paul and Jim, grew it into one of the most successful offices in the borough.

In 1982 Anita had a vision that one day 30th Avenue would be the key central spot of Astoria. That vision has become a reality, with Astoria and LIC now a vibrant, welcoming hub for young professionals and families alike.
